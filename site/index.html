      1. Call your bank's fraud line now. Give them the bank report above.
      2. Open a case at your nearest SAPS station. Bring the case pack PDF and your phone with the original chats.
      3. Don't delete the chat or block the number until the police have what they need.

      How it works

      1. ForwardChats, screenshots and proof of payment.
      2. LockEvery file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint and a timestamp.
      3. ExtractBank accounts, numbers, links and amounts are pulled out automatically.
      4. LinkScam Fingerprint matches victims by account, number, link and message script.
      5. ActCase pack for SAPS, statement in your language, report for your bank.

      Privacy and security

      What leaves your phone

      • Keyed fingerprints (HMAC-SHA256) of the scammer's bank account, phone numbers and link domains, only if you choose to add your report.
      • The amount lost, date, scam type and province, for the "you are not alone" count.

      What never leaves your phone

      • Your name, ID number or contact details.
      • Your chats, screenshots and proof of payment.
      • Readable account or phone numbers. Fingerprints can't be turned back into numbers without the server's secret key.

      This is a hackathon prototype. Matching runs in your browser against a sample database of fictional reports, and reports you add are saved only in this browser. In the full version, fingerprints are computed on a server in South Africa with a secret key, evidence is encrypted, and an independent timestamp authority proves when files were locked.
