Security by design

Built to be attacked. Checked every day.

Ubufakazi handles evidence of crimes and alerts banks to freeze accounts, so it is a target. Here is how it is protected, mapped to OWASP Top 10:2025, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, ISO/IEC 27037 and NIST SP 800-86, with live checks from this server and our honest limits.

Evidence handling: ISO/IEC 27037 and NIST SP 800-86

  1. 1

    Identify & collect

    The complainant submits chats, screenshots, slips and voice notes on the website or Telegram, with consent. Each file is identified by its bytes, not its name.

  2. 2

    Acquire & preserve

    The original is stored read-only with its SHA-256 fingerprint and an RFC 3161 timestamp from an independent authority. Every access is in the hash-chained audit log (chain of custody).

  3. 3

    Examine & analyse

    Rules and AI read working copies, never the original. Accounts, numbers and scripts are linked across station jurisdictions; payment slips are checked for signs of editing.

  4. 4

    Report

    A sealed case pack lists every file's fingerprint and timestamp. Anyone can verify the pack and each file on Verify, without uploading anything.

Threat model

Attacker wants to…Our defence
Frame an innocent person's accountOne report never blocks or warns "do not pay"; it needs several victims in two or more jurisdictions, or the account's own bank freezing it. AI-only or injection-flagged details never count.
Check whether they have been reported (a scammer snooping)No public search. The beneficiary check is for subscribed, signed banks only, answers counts not details, has quotas, and abuse raises alerts.
Trick the AI with hidden instructionsPrompt-injection shield, hardened reading prompt, AI has no powers, AI-only details stay unverified.
Forge or edit a police case packSealed PDFs: one changed character breaks the seal on Verify; unknown seals are reported as possibly forged.
Submit an edited proof of paymentPayment-slip checks flag editing software, edits after creation and appended updates, as warning signs for the officer.
Spoof or replay a bank messageHMAC signatures over timestamp and body, a 5-minute window, single-use signatures for the beneficiary check, every rejection audited and alerted.
Steal victims' dataBanks never receive names, chats or statements; station isolation; two-factor sign-in; identifiers matched by keyed hashes; consent first, under POPIA.
Misuse insider accessEvery view and download audited in a hash-chained log; bulk access raises an alert; a broken chain is a critical alert.

Honest limits

  • AI reading is done by Google Gemini, outside South Africa, and only with the complainant's consent. A production system would use an in-region AI service.
  • The demo is hosted on Render and Neon (outside South Africa). Stations, jurisdictions and the bank are fictional, and all data is synthetic.
  • Not yet independently penetration-tested. Tamper-evident, not tamper-proof: changes are detected, not prevented.
  • Prompt-injection and editing checks are rules: they raise warning signs and can miss a careful attacker. Officers check every original.
