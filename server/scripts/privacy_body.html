Privacy policy

Your evidence, your control

How Ubufakazi handles what you send through the website, Telegram or WhatsApp. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Demonstration service. Ubufakazi is a HackSecure 2026 prototype by Team CyberThreat. It is not SAPS, it is not connected to SAPS or any real bank, and its bank and branches are fictional. Please use the demo evidence kit or test data, not real evidence from a real crime. In danger now? Call 10111.

Who is responsible

Team CyberThreat (Thabang Manyama, Amani Tondi and Shaun Matlou), students taking part in HackSecure 2026 in South Africa, run this demonstration. Contact us through the project page on GitHub. Never post personal details, evidence or your receipt key there.

What we collect

What you give us

  • What happened: the type of incident, province, town, when it happened and your own description
  • Evidence you choose to send: screenshots, photos, documents, chat exports, voice notes and pasted messages
  • Optional: the name you want on your draft statement, answers such as the amount paid, and an approximate location (rounded to about 1 km)
  • Your consent, with the date and the wording you agreed to

What we never ask for

  • ID numbers (the bot refuses them)
  • Your exact GPS position, contacts or other apps' data
  • Bank logins, PINs or one-time passwords
  • On WhatsApp, your phone number is used only to reply to you while you chat. The report keeps a scrambled code (a keyed hash), not the number. On Telegram, the report keeps your Telegram chat ID so the bot can reply.

How we use it

  • To organise your evidence, fingerprint each file (SHA-256), and prepare a draft statement and case pack for you and for the bank's escalation to SAPS
  • To show your report to fraud investigators at the fictional bank branch for the area you chose, and to nobody else. Other branches see only that a restricted report exists
  • To find possible connections between reports (for example the same receiving account or phone number) by comparing keyed hashes, not raw values
  • To keep an audit log of who viewed, downloaded or changed a report

We do not sell your information, show you adverts, or contact SAPS or any bank. Public scam warnings are made only when you choose to create one, and they leave out names, account numbers and case references.

Services that process your data

  • Render (hosting) and Neon (database), both in Frankfurt, Germany
  • Meta / WhatsApp or Telegram, when you report through those apps: they carry your messages under their own privacy policies
  • Google Gemini, when AI reading is switched on: images, voice notes and scanned PDFs are sent to Google to be transcribed. The result is labelled "AI-generated, unverified" and never replaces your original file

How long we keep it

A draft you have not submitted is kept in memory only, and is cleared when you send /stop or after 2 hours of inactivity. Submitted reports are kept for the demonstration. We intend to delete them after the HackSecure 2026 event, and you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Before you submit: send /stop (or "stop") in the chat, or use Quick exit on the website. The draft is cleared and nothing from it is saved.
  • After you submit: open an issue on our GitHub page titled "Deletion request", with no personal details in it. We will reply with a private way to confirm the report is yours (using your reference and receipt key), then delete the report, its evidence files and its case pack.
  • On your phone: WhatsApp does not let a business delete messages it has sent. To remove the chat from your phone, press and hold it in your chat list, then choose Delete.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how it is used. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
