Scammed? Do this now.

The first hour matters most, especially if money is involved. Here is what to do, in order, and who to call.

1. Call your bank's fraud line

Use the number on the back of your card or in your banking app, never a number the scammer gave you. Ask them to try to stop or recall the payment. Speed matters.

2. Keep the evidence

Don't delete the chat. Export it (WhatsApp: chat › More › Export chat), keep screenshots and proof of payment. Ubufakazi fingerprints each file so you can show it hasn't been changed.

3. Open a case at a police station

Take your ID, your case pack and the evidence. Ask for the case to be registered and write down the CAS number. Fraud and cybercrime (Cybercrimes Act 19 of 2020) are criminal offences.

4. Protect yourself from round two

Scammers often come back as "recovery agents" promising to get your money back for a fee. That is another scam. Change passwords you shared and turn on two-step verification.
