For banks and fintechs

Freeze the mule account before the money moves

Scam victims report to SAPS through Ubufakazi. When several victims say they paid into the same account at your bank, Ubufakazi sends your fraud team a signed alert straight away, with the evidence summary. Your team reviews it and can block the account in one click. SAPS and the victim see your decision.

How it works

  1. 1

    Victims report

    On the website or Telegram. Every file is fingerprinted (SHA-256) and timestamped, and the report goes to the SAPS station whose jurisdiction covers where it happened.

  2. 2

    Reports are linked

    Ubufakazi finds reports that name the same receiving account, phone number, website or message script, across every station and province.

  3. 3

    Your bank gets an alert

    Over a signed API: the account number, how many separate reports name it, amounts, dates, provinces and scam types. Never victims' names, ID numbers or chats.

  4. 4

    You block the account

    Investigate, freeze, lift the freeze or close. Each decision goes back to Ubufakazi, where the investigating officer and the victim can see it.

What your bank never receives

  • Victims' names, ID numbers, phone numbers, chats or statements. Alerts are about the receiving account only.
  • A verdict. An alert is an investigative lead: the account may belong to a money mule or a compromised customer, so your team checks its own records before acting.
